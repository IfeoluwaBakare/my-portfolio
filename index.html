RFM Customer Segmentation

Coursework · Databricks (PySpark + SQL)
Complete
DatabricksPySparkSQLAWS S3Lakeview Dashboard

The problem: The business needed to understand which customers were worth retaining and which were at risk of becoming inactive.

What I did: Analyzed over 30,000 sales transactions using SQL and PySpark in Databricks, scoring each customer on recency, frequency, and monetary value, then grouped them into ten segments to guide marketing decisions.

What I found: 47% of customers were dormant, while only 55 were newly acquired, pointing toward a stronger opportunity in customer reactivation than acquisition.

Coca-Cola vs. PepsiCo: Financial Ratio Analysis

Managerial accounting coursework
Complete
Financial Statement AnalysisLiquidity RatiosProfitability Ratios

The question: Which company, Coca-Cola or PepsiCo, showed stronger financial health based on its financial statements?

What I did: Calculated and compared 14 ratios covering liquidity, leverage, efficiency, and profitability for both companies, then rebuilt the analysis as a linked Excel model so every ratio traces back to its source.

What I found: The analysis favored Coca-Cola overall, with stronger liquidity, lower leverage, and higher profitability across several measures. PepsiCo led on inventory efficiency, but Coca-Cola's broader financial position was the stronger case.
